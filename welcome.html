Why it exists

Every crayon. No box.

Executive function is the cardboard box. In a neurotypical brain, that box holds working memory, task initiation, and emotional regulation in a neat little row, so they can reach in and pull out exactly the one they need, right when they need it.

When you have executive dysfunction, you still have the exact same crayons: the intelligence, the intentions, the raw cognitive ability. You aren't missing any colors. You are just missing the box.

So when you try to do something simple, like pay a bill, clean a room, or reply to an email, the crayons are rolling off the desk, hiding under the couch, snapping in half. You end up spending 90% of your energy rounding them up, and you're exhausted before you've started to draw.

Nenemi exists to be the box. Not a better to-do list. Not a coach. The cardboard. So the 90% goes back to the drawing.

What's inside
Say it anywhere.Type it or talk it, messy is fine. Nenemi files it in the right room, starts a new one, or holds it loose. You never decide where it goes.
Rooms.One thing per room, always visible, never lost. Each one opens with where you left off and one small next move.
My day.Dump the whole day in one breath and get a usable day back, with air between things. Or write about it. A page a day, yours only.
A way back in.When everything is everywhere: four doors, then one small move. Never a list. Never a lecture.
What it never does
  • Tells you what to do. It offers, asks, or holds.
  • Keeps score. No streaks, no red, no overdue, no "still up?"
  • Makes you organize before you can dump.
  • Pretends to be a person. Nenemi is the nudge, not the treatment. Real people heal, and the app points to them.
The name

Nenemi is Nahuatl for "to walk, to wander." Your brain isn't broken. It's high-spec hardware running in the wrong environment.
