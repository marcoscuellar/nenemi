Where this landed

Same ribbon silhouette as the reference — thick rounded bars, edge-to-edge, almost no margin — just without the gradient shading, so it's one file for the favicon, the app icon, and the splash screen instead of three different treatments.

Ribbon keeps the teal fold detail for a bit of dimension at larger sizes. Ribbon Solid drops that and is just teal, full stop — the boldest, simplest version, and my pick if you want it to read as a color block first and a letterform second.
