# NENEMI — "AI helps, humans heal" (principle + Humans Heal feature)

## The principle
NENEMI is the nudge, not the treatment. It helps you *move* — capture, re-enter, get unstuck, stay with a task. But it is explicitly **not** a doctor, therapist, or coach, and it never pretends to be. The real healing happens with real people. This is a core ethos, not a disclaimer bolted on: it keeps NENEMI honest, protects users, and builds trust.

Tone rules that follow from it:
- Meds are framed as a **real, valid option** — never pushed, never stigmatized. A professional helps you decide.
- No fake urgency, no guilt, no "you should" — same shame-free voice as the rest of NENEMI.
- When something is heavy, NENEMI points to immediate human support (988) rather than trying to handle it itself.

## The "Humans heal" page (built)
Reached from the home dropdown (5th item). Calm, non-clinical. Cards:
- **Find an ADHD coach** — ADDA · CHADD
- **Talk to a therapist** — Psychology Today finder
- **Ask about medication** — "Meds are a real, valid option. A psychiatrist can help you decide."
- **Heavy right now?** — highlighted: "Call or text 988 — free, 24/7." (US Suicide & Crisis Lifeline)
- Footer: "NENEMI isn't a doctor or a therapist — it's the nudge, not the treatment. The real healing happens with real people."

Resources used are real and reputable (ADDA add.org, CHADD chadd.org, Psychology Today, 988).

## Business direction — coach partnerships (Marcos, Aug 2026)
Marcos plans to **partner with specific, vetted ADHD coaches** to be NENEMI's "go-to professionals." Instead of only linking to open directories, the Humans Heal page would surface a curated network of trusted coaches NENEMI has a relationship with.

Implications / open questions to design later:
- **Curated network vs. open directory:** likely a hybrid — feature partner coaches first, keep ADDA/CHADD/Psychology Today as the broader fallback so no one's stuck if a partner isn't a fit.
- **Vetting bar:** what makes a coach a NENEMI "go-to" — ADHD specialization, credentials (e.g., ICF / ADHD-specific certs like ADDCA), lived experience, shame-free approach that matches NENEMI's ethos.
- **Business model:** referral relationship, revenue share, or sponsored placement — must be disclosed to users to keep trust (transparency is on-brand).
- **Matching:** eventually, could match a user to a coach by fit (schedule, style, focus area) rather than a flat list.
- **Safety/ethics:** clear that partners are independent professionals; NENEMI facilitates the intro, doesn't provide care. Keep the 988 / crisis path always present and never gated behind a partner.

## Why it matters (positioning)
This is a differentiator: most productivity/ADHD apps either ignore professional help or bury it. NENEMI makes the human handoff a first-class, curated part of the product — reinforcing "you're not alone in this" (the *dos* thesis: you + support), and opening a real, trust-first revenue path.
