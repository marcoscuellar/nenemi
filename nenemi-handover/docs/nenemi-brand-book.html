01 · Story & purpose

Why NENEMI exists

Read this first. If a design or a word ever feels off, it broke something on this page.

You aren't missing any colors. You are just missing the box.

Executive function is the cardboard box. It holds working memory, task initiation, and emotional regulation in a neat row, so you can reach in and pull out the one you need.

With executive dysfunction you still have the exact same crayons: the intelligence, the intentions, the raw ability. But when you try to do something simple, like pay a bill or reply to an email, the crayons roll off the desk, hide under the couch, snap in half. You spend 90% of your energy rounding them up, and you're exhausted before you've started to draw.

Nenemi exists to be the box. Not a better to-do list. Not a coach. The cardboard. So the 90% goes back to the drawing.

The one test

Does this hand them a crayon, or make them go find one?

Every feature, word, and pixel is judged against it. If it adds a decision, a step, a setting, or a wall between the person and putting something down, it's wrong, even if it's clever.

The name

The name
NENEMI
neh-NEH-mee

Nenemi comes from Nahuatl, a living Indigenous language spoken by Nahua communities in Mexico. The word means “to walk” or “to go about.” We chose it because your path doesn’t have to be straight to keep moving.

Mind-wandering is not a moral failure. Nonlinear thinking is an exploratory strength, not a broken compass.

Nenemi isn't built to force a wandering mind into a straight line. It walks beside you, holds the context, and keeps your place, so wandering off never means losing the trail.

This is the exact card people see on onboarding screen 19b, and it mirrors the mynenemi.com footer. The name is spoken neh-NEH-mee, and written NÈNÈMI in onboarding copy. The wordmark is always NENEMI.

What the box means in the product

Capture is the lid

Say anything, anywhere, and it's held. Nobody decides where it goes first.

Rooms are compartments

One thing per room, always visible, never lost. Object permanence, outsourced.

The Brief is the hand

It pulls out exactly what's needed: where you left off, and the next small move.

The day is the row

Only what's realistic, with air between things. No shame, just math.

The doors say “I've got it”

When everything spills: four ways back in, then one small move.

Humans heal

The box is the nudge, not the treatment. We point to real people and never pretend to be one.

02 · The mark

The N & lockups

The shape is locked. Use the files in photos/logo/ exactly, so people learn it as ours.

One stepped N: terraced steps on one leg, a sure diagonal, soft rounded corners. It lives in two colors only, ink and white.

The terraces nod to the Aztec xicalcoliuhqui, the stepped fret carved at Teotihuacan and Templo Mayor. Stone steps you climb, leave, and come back to, and they stay exactly as they were.

Wordmark, clear space, size

The wordmark is Archivo Black in spaced caps: NENEMI. It's tracked +0.14em in running layouts and +0.36em under the mark on the splash. It's never condensed, never lightweight, never in color.

Leave clear space of one terrace (one step of the mark) on every side. The smallest symbol size is 20px, and it still reads as the N at favicon scale.

Do & don't

✓Solid ink on white or ground.
✓White on ink.
✕Don't stretch or squash.
✕Don't put it on color. Not teal, not maíz, not a gradient. (v3 allowed teal and maíz tiles; v4 retires them.)
03 · Color

Strictly monochrome

Protect attention. Every extra hue is a sensory tax, and a colored dot asks to be looked at. The app is ink, paper, and carbon. Color is a guest, and it only visits two places.

Ink does the work. Paper holds it. Carbon says "quiet" and "done." That's the whole app.

Always dark

NENEMI
The splash

Every load, for everyone. The mark and wordmark fade in over 400ms, hold 1000ms, then fade out over 400ms into the Front Door (about 1.8s). A tap or Enter/Space/Esc skips it.

The dark sanctuary

Stuck, Focus, Friction, and Human support are dark in every theme. No glare when someone is frozen. Accents are light carbon.

Motion

Everything reads as returning, not loading. Onboarding cards: a 220ms fade with a 6px rise. Act changes: .4s. Toasts: 200ms slide-and-fade. Nothing bounces or flashes. Under reduced motion, cards appear still.

08 · How the brand behaves

The non-negotiables

The box never keeps score, never commands, and never explains itself from inside.

Never tell them what to do

Copy offers, asks, or holds. No imperatives aimed at the person. "Dump it here" is the one exception: it's the box's name for its own lid.

No shame mechanics, ever

No streaks, no red, no "overdue", no countdowns, no comments on the hour.

Capture before organize

Say it first, sort it never. The app files it, offers a room, or holds it loose.

Re-entry over planning

The first thing on any screen is where you left off and one small move, not a list.

One small move

Stuck screens offer one thing. Never more options than the four doors.

The box is quiet

No tutorials, tooltips, or manifesto inside the app. One quiet intro line for signed-out visitors is the ceiling.

Always a way out

From a conversation, Place is one tap away. From anywhere, "I'm stuck" is one tap away. Nobody is trapped in a loop.

Humans heal

Nenemi is the nudge, not the treatment. Point to real people; never pretend to be one.

09 · Voice

How NENEMI speaks

People with ADHD have heard "you're not trying hard enough" their whole lives. One wrong nag and we become one more voice that let them down.

Warm, short, specific. A friend who happens to be a genius organizer: not a therapist, not a coach, not cold software.

Clinical "ADHD tool" framing

Rules for every line

One sentence per line. Proper capitalization and punctuation. No exclamation marks. Cut before adding. The win we deliver is "oh, I know exactly where I was," not "I finished everything." Plans are "Full access" and "Free · n of 2 rooms", and the paywall says "Unlimited rooms."

What changed from v3

  • Idea: "The Return" became the box. The name now means to walk, to go about, as on screen 19b and the site footer.
  • Color: strictly monochrome. Cyan, mint, and green are gone. Signature teal left the app and now lives only on the 1b and 19b kickers.
  • Completion: an ink check and carbon strike-through, like a paper notebook. No mint.
  • Maíz: still for returning, but never counted. "3 days in a row" is retired, because a streak is a score.
  • The mark: ink and white only. Teal and maíz tiles are retired. The mark is now one exact vector, rebuilt from the original artwork, and every copy (this book, photos/logo/, the splash, 19b, the app header, favicon, home-screen and iPhone icons, the landing page) uses it.
  • Shape: 12px cards, 8px controls, pill navigation, hairlines only (v3 used 18–22px cards and 100px buttons).
  • Type: Inter for UI, Archivo 700 for page titles, and Archivo Black 900 for the Front Door and the onboarding hooks.
  • New: the Front Porch and Place, the three onboarding acts, the mid-conversation escape, and the QA sweep.
