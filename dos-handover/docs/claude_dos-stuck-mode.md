# DOS — "I'm Stuck" Sanctuary (feature)

Added Aug 2026, inspired by Marcos's ŌLLIN AI splash concept (calm black screen, single glowing input, "ready when you are").

## The moment it's for
It's 1pm. Marcos is stressed, can't start a project, executive-function frozen. He opens DOS and says "hey, I'm stuck." The critical rule: **opening the app must NOT be a backlog ambush** ("oh f*ck, I have so much to do"). Instead it's a *return-home / sanctuary* moment that hands him ONE small, non-pharmaceutical nudge to get unstuck.

## Behavior
- **Triggers:** typing stuck-language in the composer ("I'm stuck," "can't start," "overwhelmed," "paralyzed," "don't know where to start," "too much"), OR a quiet "Feeling stuck? we got you →" link under the composer on Home.
- **Short-circuits everything** — no routing, no room-filing, and it never surfaces the task list. Can't accidentally land on the backlog.
- **Calm dark space** (deliberately dark in both themes — a mood shift from the productive white home): near-black, soft lime radial glow, a small ō-style mark. Green is used sparingly, one accent (the ŌLLIN principle).
- **One nudge at a time** from a rotating set of non-pharma ADHD interventions: stand up & shake out 15s, open+name the file only, 2-minute timer (quit at 2:01), open a draft and write one silly sentence, say the first tiny step out loud, drink water first, one song, change your spot, body-double ("I'll sit with you"), ask future-you the one easier thing.
- **Actions:** "Did it" → warm confirmation ("That's a real start. Proud of you."); "give me another" → rotate; "just breathe with me" → 30-sec breathing ring (body-double / grounding).

## Why it matters (positioning)
This is DOS's *return-home* pillar and a strong differentiator: it's not "here's your list," it's "here's one small move." Directly serves the anti-guilt, shame-free, no-fake-urgency principles. Pairs with Focus/Friction Pause (help you *stay*) — this one helps you *start*.

## Design note
Reserve heavy green for this and other "help/reward" moments; the rest of the app stays black-and-white-driven per Marcos's direction. Fully tokenized; the sanctuary's dark palette is intentional and fixed (not theme-flipping).
