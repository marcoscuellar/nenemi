# DOS — what the name means

**DOS = "two" in Spanish.** It's *you + the AI* — the two of you, together.

The thesis in one word: **the AI catches you when you can't finish on your own.** You're not alone with the executive-function wall. There's a second one in it with you.

Everything in the product serves this:
- The **"I'm stuck" sanctuary** — the AI catches you mid-freeze and hands you one small move.
- The **Brief** ("where you left off") — the AI holds your context so you don't have to reconstruct it.
- The **Friction Pause** — catches you before you drift off a task, without a wall.
- **Capture** — you dump messy, the AI files it. Two halves of one motion.

Earlier build was called **COS** (Cognitive Operating System) — same soul ("continue where you left off"), different name. Merged into DOS: keep the DOS name, sage palette, and Archivo/Inter/JetBrains Mono fonts; carry forward COS's architecture and features.
