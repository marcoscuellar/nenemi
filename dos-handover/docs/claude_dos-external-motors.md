# DOS — External Motors (ADHD non-pharmaceutical support library)

**What it is:** the family of ways an ADHD brain borrows structure from outside itself, because the inside doesn't reliably supply it. Body doubling is one of many. Source material for the stuck / Regroup nudges. NOT medical advice — pairs *with* real care (see dos-humans-heal), never instead of it.

Scannable version published as an Artifact ("External Motors", favicon ⚙️). **Marcos has ~3 years of personal research to merge on top of this — his findings win; this v1 fills gaps.**

Rule (from the COS Regroup engine): every nudge should map to a motor here and be tagged with the ADHD practice it operationalizes — real interventions matched to state, never one-size-fits-all.

## 01 · Starting (past the initiation wall)
- **Body doubling** — someone present (real/virtual) externalizes focus. *Already in COS.* DOS: "I'll sit here with you" / coworking match.
- **Micro-steps below resistance** — shrink task smaller than the resistance. *Already in COS ("can't start").*
- **2-minute rule** — commit to 2 min, quit at 2:01 allowed.
- **Temptation bundling** — pair hard task with something pleasant (song, coffee, spot).
- **Launch rituals / countdowns** — a fixed physical cue = "begin" (5-4-3-2-1, stand).

## 02 · Time (make it visible; ADHD sees only now / not-now)
- **Visual / analog timers** — time you can see draining.
- **Time-boxing** — fixed slots with hard edges (Today planner).
- **Flexible pomodoro** — bounded sprints + reset; keep flexible for ADHD.
- **Anchoring** — tie tasks to anchors ("after coffee," "before 3pm") not clock times (DayPlan `when`).

## 03 · Focus & friction (shape the environment)
- **Friction design** — lower friction to start good, raise it on distractions.
- **Single-tasking / one-thing** — cap to one must. *Already in COS ("too much").*
- **Externalizing object permanence** — keep current thing visible or it stops existing (Brief/Rooms).
- **Context reinstatement** — drop back into exact prior context. *Already in COS ("it broke") + Re-entry.*

## 04 · Accountability (borrow an outside expectation)
- **External accountability** — tell a person, check-ins, standing time.
- **A coach in your corner** — real ADHD coach; strongest motor. *Humans Heal + coach-partner network.*
- **Real deadlines** — tie to a person/event so they don't slip.

## 05 · Novelty & momentum (frequent small wins for a dopamine-hungry brain)
- **Gamification** — beat the timer, points, tiny rewards.
- **Tiny wins / done-list** — track what you did, not what's left; no red overdue.
- **Novelty rotation** — rotate tools/spots before the novelty cliff (7–14 days).
- **Celebrate the start** — reinforce showing up. *Already stuck-mode close.*

## 06 · Body & energy (regulation first)
- **Movement to activate** — stand, shake out, walk. *Already a stuck nudge.*
- **Basic needs first** — water, food, meds, bathroom, temp; dysregulation ≠ laziness.
- **Rest as a valid plan** — permission to stop, everything held. *Already COS ("just tired").*
- **Sensory regulation** — noise-cancelling, fidget, music, dim light (sanctuary + breathing ring).

## 07 · Shame & emotion (the invisible motor-killer)
- **Self-compassion over self-criticism** — kindness unfreezes; the whole DOS voice.
- **Name the feeling** — labeling drains its charge. *Already the Friction Pause.*
- **No shame, no streaks, no debt** — anti-guilt rollover. *Zero-Shame Purge — still to build, biggest wedge.*
- **Externalize the inner critic** — separate "the ADHD" from "me"; neutral logs.

## 08 · Externalizing memory (a brain outside the brain — DOS's core)
- **Frictionless capture** — one box/voice, catch the thought before it vanishes. *The composer.*
- **Second brain / context store** — hold context so you never reconstruct. *Rooms + Brief — the thesis.*
- **Offload open loops** — writing unfinished things down frees mental RAM (Zeigarnik).
