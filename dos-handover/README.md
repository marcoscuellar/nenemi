# Nenemi

> **Nenemi** *(verb, Nahuatl)*: To walk, to travel, to wander.

## What's in the Name?

Standard productivity software assumes the human brain operates like a conveyor belt: rigid, linear, and predictable. For neurodivergent minds, that model produces friction, transition fatigue, and shame spirals.

We named this project **Nenemi**—a Nahuatl word meaning *to wander* or *to walk a journey*—to reframe how we approach executive function:

- **Mind-wandering is not a moral failure:** Nonlinear thinking is an exploratory strength, not a broken compass.
- **An External Motor:** When executive dysfunction triggers "stuck mode," willpower isn't the solution. Nenemi acts as an external neurological prosthetic to reduce friction and spark momentum.
- **Companion, not a taskmaster:** Rather than enforcing rigid streaks and punishment, Nenemi walks beside you, holding context and gently guiding you back to your path.

Nenemi isn't built to force a wandering mind into a straight line; it's built to support the journey.
