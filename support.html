Your data

Export everything from the account menu inside the app, any time. To delete your account and all of it, email the address above. Gone within 30 days.

Something didn't save

Nenemi saves to your device first, then syncs. If a note is missing on one device, open the app on the device where you wrote it, wait a few seconds with a connection, and it will come across.

The microphone isn't working

Speech is handled by your phone or browser. On iPhone, Settings, Safari, Microphone, and allow mynenemi.com. On Android, allow the microphone when Chrome asks.

Heavy right now

Nenemi is not a crisis service. In the United States, call or text 988, any hour. A human answers.
